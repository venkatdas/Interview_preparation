# Core 75 Problems

A short list of 75 classic interview problems that together cover every Essential pattern. Each
row links to the problem page **inside this curriculum** (statement with staged hints) and to its
tested solution. It plays the role of the well-known "Blind 75" style lists, mapped to our folders.

**How to use it**

1. Work through it after the topic it belongs to (see [STUDY_PLAN_12_WEEKS.md](STUDY_PLAN_12_WEEKS.md)).
2. Tick a box only when you solved the problem **without looking at a solution**, within 35 minutes.
3. Add every solved problem to your review log and re-solve it on the [spaced-repetition schedule](SPACED_REPETITION_SCHEDULE.md).
4. Ready check: all 75 ticked, and at least 60 re-solved after a week.
